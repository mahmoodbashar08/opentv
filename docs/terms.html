4. How the rules are enforced

Every comment carries a report control, and every profile can be reported and blocked. You can report content as spam, harassment, hate speech, sexual content, violence, an unmarked spoiler, or something else.

  • Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.
  • Content is hidden automatically once enough separate people report it, before any human has looked at it — so the worst material stops being visible quickly rather than eventually.
  • Blocking works in both directions. Someone you block cannot see your profile or your comments, and you will not see theirs, anywhere in the app.
  • Accounts that post objectionable content are terminated, and we may remove any content and end any account at our discretion where these terms are broken.

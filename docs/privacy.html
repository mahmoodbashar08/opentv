Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

The short version: everything stays on your device. We collect nothing.

What data OpenTV stores

Your library — shows, movies, watch history, ratings, emotions, comments, lists, profile details and photos — is stored in a local database on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. OpenTV has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Network requests

The app makes network requests only to The Movie Database (TMDB), to fetch show and movie information and artwork. These requests contain only the title or id being looked up — never your identity or your library. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Importing your TV Time export

When you import a TV Time GDPR export, the file is read and processed entirely on your device. During import the app may download images referenced inside your export (your avatar, cover and comment photos) from TV Time's servers, saving them locally so they survive TV Time's shutdown.

Your data, your control

You can export your complete library at any time from Settings — as a TV Time-format ZIP or a raw JSON backup — and you can erase everything with one tap. Deleting the app deletes all data with it.

Children

OpenTV does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

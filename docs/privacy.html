Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

The short version: your library stays on your device. The tracker needs no account and no server. There is now an optional community you can join — if you join it, the things you publish (your handle, your comments, your ratings) are stored on a server. Your watch history never is.

What data OpenTV stores on your phone

Your library — shows, movies, watch history, ratings, emotions, comments, lists, profile details and photos — is stored in a local database on your phone. That database is the app's only source of truth. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is not backed up to us: the optional iCloud backup goes to your own iCloud account, which we cannot read. There are no analytics and no advertising.

The OpenTV community — optional, and off unless you join

OpenTV can connect to a community where people comment on episodes, rate them, follow each other and publish lists. Joining is entirely optional. If you never join, the app never contacts our server at all, and it works exactly as described above — every screen, every statistic, every feature of the tracker.

If you do join, by signing in with Apple or Google, the following is stored on our server:

  • a profile: your handle, display name, avatar, bio and links, and whether your profile is private;
  • an identity record from Apple or Google that lets you sign back in. We receive an opaque account identifier from them — not your Apple or Google password;
  • the comments and episode ratings you post through the community, and the likes you give;
  • who you follow, who follows you, and anyone you block;
  • lists you choose to publish;
  • your notifications, and any reports you file or that are filed about your content.

If you imported a TV Time export, the app can also offer to bring your old TV Time comments across, and to reconnect you with the friends you had there. Both are offered and neither happens by itself. The reconnection sends only the numeric TV Time friend identifiers that were already inside your own export, so people who held the matching identifier can be matched with you.

What is never sent to our server, whether or not you join: your watch history — no episode, no movie, no progress, no dates; the TV Time export file itself; the TV Time email address or password; your imported private comments unless you explicitly choose to publish them; your library statistics; your location; your contacts. The server has no table for a watched episode, and that is a design decision, not an omission: if the server disappeared tomorrow, you would keep your complete history and the app would still work.

The community server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), which processes and stores this data on our behalf. Cloudflare also keeps standard, short-lived request logs, which may include an IP address.

Network requests

The app fetches show and movie information and artwork from TheTVDB and The Movie Database (TMDB). These requests contain only the title or id being looked up — never your identity and never your library. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

If you have joined the community, the app also talks to the OpenTV community server described above. If you have not, it does not.

Importing your TV Time export

When you import a TV Time GDPR export, the file is read and processed entirely on your device. During import the app may download images referenced inside your export (your avatar, cover and comment photos) from TV Time's servers, saving them locally so they survive TV Time's shutdown.

Your data, your control

You can export your complete library at any time from Settings — as a TV Time-format ZIP or a raw JSON backup — and you can erase everything with one tap. Deleting the app deletes all local data with it.

If you have joined the community, Settings → Account offers two separate things:

  • Leave the community signs this device out. Nothing is deleted — your profile and your comments remain, and you can sign back in whenever you like.
  • Delete my community account permanently removes your community presence: the sign-in identity (so signing in again creates a new, empty profile), your comments, likes and ratings, your follows and blocks, your published lists and your notifications. Your profile record is stripped of all personal data immediately and the empty shell is purged afterwards. Your library, watch history and imported data stay on your phone — deleting the community account does not touch them.

If you would rather ask us, write to the address below and we will delete the account for you.

Children

The tracker collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The community contains content written by other people and is not intended for children under 13; you must be at least 13 to join it.
